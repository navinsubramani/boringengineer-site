To escape the winter chill in New Hampshire, we decided to embark on a tropical adventure to Puerto Rico and its surrounding islands. Our journey took us to three stunning islands: Puerto Rico, Culebra, and Vieques. Each island offered its own unique charm and experiences, making our winter escape truly unforgettable.

<!-- truncate -->

**Below is our itinerary for this tropical getaway:**
1. Land in San Juan, Puerto Rico and stayed at a beautiful beach resort at Aquatika.
2. Spent a day at Culenbra Island - doing snorkeling at Tamarindo Beach and relaxing at Flamenco Beach.
3. Walked around Old San Juan, exploring its colorful streets and historic sites.
4. Took a ferry to Vieques Island - visited the Bioluminescent Bay at night and enjoyed the serene beaches during the day.
5. Indulged in local Puerto Rican cuisine, savoring dishes like mofongo and fresh seafood.
6. Visited the El Yunque National Forest to experience the zip-lining.

Once we landed in San Juan and rented a car, I headed straight to the Aquatika resort. The resort was nestled along the coast, offering stunning views of the ocean and easy access to the beach. My first few realizations were,
- the cost of living is quite low here compared to the mainland US, like the car rental is $6 a day
- I felt the roads are narrow in rural places like Indian roads, but Indian roads are better maintained compartaively. Maybe its a island thing.
- the people are super friendly and welcoming, making me feel at home instantly.
- You find lot of local resstaurants and street food vendors offering delicious Puerto Rican cuisine.

It could be personal, but I had the best experience in Culebra Island. The ferry ride from Puerto Rico to Culebra was smooth and the guide in the port were super informative. We took a 7AM ferry to Culebra, so we can beat the crowd and get to the snorkeling spot early. We did snorkeling at Tamarindo Beach, which is known for its crystal-clear waters and vibrant marine life. Swimming alongside colorful fish and coral reefs was an unforgettable experience. After snorkeling, we headed to Flamenco Beach, often ranked as one of the best beaches in the world. The white sandy shores and turquoise waters were simply breathtaking. We spent the afternoon lounging on the beach, soaking up the sun, and enjoying the serene surroundings.

This is a small island, so you can easily explore it in a day. We rented a golf cart to get around the island, which was a fun and convenient way to see the sights.

We spent a day exploring Old San Juan. The colorful colonial buildings, cobblestone streets, and historic forts created a charming atmosphere. Finding parking was a challenge, and the walk is a bit uphill in some places, but the experience was worth it.

We visited a cafe shop called `Delicadas` which had amazing coffee and pastries. The young lady working there was super friendly and gave us great recommendations for places to visit in Old San Juan. We found a local photographer with Macaws and got some amazing pictures clicked. This is a relazing place to walk around and soak in the culture.

We then took a ferry to Vieques Island, known for its pristine beaches and the famous Bioluminescent Bay. The island is 3x the size of Culebra, so we rented a car to explore it (had a bad experience with the renatl company so I recommend you do your research before renting). We visited the Bioluminescent Bay at night, and it was a magical experience. The water glowed with bioluminescent organisms, and we were lucky to have a clear sky that night, making the experience even more enchanting. During the day, we visited some of the beaches on Vieques, such as Sun Bay Beach but the best is the bioluminescent bay itself.

We were not able to do the hiking in the rain forest as there was some road construction going on, but we did spend good time in a running river nearby, catching fish and enjoying the natural surroundings. The zip-lining experience was thrilling - the guides were professional and fun to interact with.

**Roaming around**:

- We visited the T-mobile city in San Juan. There is a small shop called `Dulicnea Ice Cream and Churros` which had a amazing Churros.
- The Aquatika resort had good homes with kitchen, so we cooked some meals ourselves. The local grocery stores had fresh produce and seafoods. The resort also had a private beach area, lazy river, and multiple pools with hot tubs.
- Driving around Puerto Rico coast line is nice. The views of the ocean is very relaxing.


Overall going from 30F to 85F in December was a great winter escape. I would recommend this trip to anyone looking for a tropical getaway. Cheers.!
